===== Detailed Description =====

🎵 Video Controller - Speed, Pitch & A-B Loop for YouTube

Slow it down, change the key, and loop the hard part.
Made for music practice, language learning and lectures.


🆕 What's New in v4.4.0

✅ Speed, pitch and loop are fully independent: change one and the other two stay exactly as set
✅ Loops play smoothly: no more stuttering or getting stuck when you turn a loop on
✅ Clean pitch shifting: no more crackling noise when raising the pitch
✅ "Now" buttons: set loop points A and B at the current playback position
✅ Keyboard shortcuts: change speed and set A/B without opening the popup
✅ Everything fits on one screen: no more scrolling in the popup
✅ The popup connects instantly and shows your current settings when reopened
✅ Seeking within a video no longer resets your settings


✨ Key Features

🚀 Playback Speed
• 0.25x to 4.0x
• −0.2 / −0.1 / +0.1 / +0.2 buttons for fine control
• Presets: 60%, 70%, 80%, 90%, 110%, 120%
• Click the current speed to reset to 1.0x
• Your speed holds even if the YouTube player tries to change it


🎵 Pitch (Key)
• Shift up or down by semitones, from −24 to +24 (two octaves)
• −2 / −1 / +1 / +2 semitone buttons, plus whole-tone presets (−2T, −1T, +1T, +2T)
• Changes the key without changing the speed
• Click the current pitch to return to the original key


🔁 A-B Loop
• Press "Now" to set A or B at the current position (setting B starts the loop)
• Or type a time ("1:30" or "90"), or drag the handles on the slider
• Plays all the way to B, then jumps back to A
• Never skips to the next video, even in autoplay playlists
• Yellow marker shows the current playback position


⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts
• Speed up / down: Alt(⌥) + Shift + . / ,
• Set loop A: Alt(⌥) + Shift + A
• Set loop B and start looping: Alt(⌥) + Shift + B
• Pitch up/down and loop on/off can be assigned in chrome://extensions/shortcuts
• A small on-screen message confirms each change, even in fullscreen


🎛️ Master ON/OFF Switch
• Turn every feature off at once to hear the original video
• Turn it back on to restore your settings
• The ON/OFF state is remembered


🔄 Automatic Reset on New Videos
• When you move to a different video, settings return to defaults
• Seeking, looping or rewinding within the same video keeps your settings


💡 Use Cases

🎸 Music Practice
• Loop a difficult passage with A-B and slow it down
• Move the key to fit your voice or instrument

📚 Language Learning
• Repeat a sentence until you catch every word
• Slow down fast speakers

🎓 Lectures
• Replay the key part of an explanation
• Speed through slower sections


📖 How to Use

1. Play a YouTube video
2. Click the extension icon
3. Adjust speed, pitch and loop in the popup, or use the keyboard shortcuts


⚠️ Notes

• Works on YouTube only
• Some DRM-protected content may not be supported
• While pitch control is active, the video's own audio is muted and the pitch-shifted audio plays instead
• After updating the extension, refresh your YouTube tab once


🔒 Privacy

• No user data is collected
• Only the master ON/OFF setting is saved, and only on your device
• Nothing is sent anywhere
